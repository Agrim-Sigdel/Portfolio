# Project Media Recording Checklist

This checklist outlines the specific screenshots (SS) and short videos to record for each of your projects to populate the portfolio's media bucket. 

> **Pro Tip:** Keep videos short (15-40 seconds) and fast-paced. Use standard dimensions (e.g., 16:9 for web, 9:16 for mobile). For CLI tools, terminal recordings look best.

---

### 1. Feriaau (Multi-Vendor Loyalty Platform)
**📸 Screenshots:**
- [ ] Landing/Marketing page hero section.
- [ ] Vendor Dashboard (showing analytics and points).
- [ ] Customer View (showing their points/rewards).
- [ ] Architecture/Setup snippet (or POS integration screen).
**🎥 Videos:**
- [ ] **30s Walkthrough:** Log in as a vendor, create/edit a reward, then switch to a customer view and redeem those points.

### 2. peeq (AI Virtual Try-On Commerce)
